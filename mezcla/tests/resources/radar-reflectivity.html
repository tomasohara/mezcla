Intuition for Radar Reflectivity Z

Explanation.

The reflectivity factor Z is defined as the integral shown above: it sums contributions from raindrops of all sizes.

The function N(D) = N₀ e−ΛD is the drop size distribution — it describes how many drops of diameter D exist per unit volume per unit size interval. Small drops are common, while large drops are exponentially rarer.

Each drop’s radar return scales as D6. Because of this sixth‑power dependence, larger drops dominate the total reflectivity, even though they are fewer in number.

The solid curves show the quantity being integrated (N(D)·D6) for light and heavy rain. The dotted curves show the underlying drop distributions. Heavy rain contains relatively more large drops, which dramatically increases Z.
